## What this app does
A location-based scavenger hunt. The user navigates to GPS waypoints in sequence. As they get closer, the background color shifts red → yellow → green and the egg icon shakes with increasing intensity. On arrival, they tap the egg to reveal a clue, then tap again to advance to the next stop.

## Notes
* "chick" refers to the emoji which appears when the user in range / has arrived at the waypoint
